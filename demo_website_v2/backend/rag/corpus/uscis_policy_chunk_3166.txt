An applicant who claims entry prior to July 1, 1924, but is only able to establish presence at some
point after that date and before January 1, 1972 can only be granted permanent residence as of the
date of the application’ s approval. If the applicant is able to establish presence prior to July 1, 1924
after a decision has already been made, the application should be reopened on a Service motion and
a new decision issued.[33]
4. Denials
If no record is found of the applicant’ s entry and the applicant is unable to establish eligibility for
registry , an of ficer must deny the application and provide a written explanation of the reasons the
application was denied.[34] There is no appeal of a denied registry application. If removal proceedings
are initiated against the applicant, then the applicant may renew the registry application in removal
proceedings.[35]

In certain cases, conditions in the applicant’ s Home Country , such as war or civil unrest, may make
compliance with the 2-year residence requirement ef fectively impossible. Additionally , some countries
have periodically imposed travel bans that have made traveling to the Home Country ef fectively
impossible. In other cases, the applicant’ s Home Country is now part of another country due to shifting
borders or other political changes. USCIS considers these circumstances, on a case-by-case basis, in
consultation with the Department of State.

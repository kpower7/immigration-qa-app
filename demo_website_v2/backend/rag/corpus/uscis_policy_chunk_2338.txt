1. Eligible Military Members
For the purpose of Form I-751 , a military member is any U.S. citizen or lawful permanent resident
currently stationed or deployed on of ficial orders as a member of the U.S. armed forces, including:  
Active duty;  
Selected Reserve of the Ready Reserve member; or  
Activated reservists and mobilized National Guardsmen.[33]
2. Evidence of Military Service
USCIS reviews every properly filed petition for the following:
Evidence that the petition involves an active duty military member;
Evidence establishing that the active duty military member is stationed or deployed overseas on
official orders; and
Evidence establishing a bona fide qualifying marriage exists.
USCIS considers military-specific evidence as strong evidence towards establishing the bona fides of
the qualifying marriage. Such evidence may include, but is not limited to:
All pages of the service member ’s Application for Identification Card/DEERS Enrollment (Form
DD-1 172-2), naming dependents;
Dependent’ s Military Identification and Privilege Card;
Certificate of Overseas Assignment to Support Application to File Petition for Naturalization
(Form DD-1278);
Copy of Permanent Change of Station Orders issued to the service member for permanent tour
of duty overseas that specifically name the spouse and children (if any);
Designation of the beneficiary on the Service Member ’s Group Life Insurance policy;
Evidence of a Family Service Members’  Group Life Insurance policy;
Evidence of the military member ’s health insurance policy on behalf of the dependent;
Documentation showing that the spouse and child (if any) reside in military base or post housing;
Powers-of-attorney of life insurance designation (general or specific);
Military TRICARE medical ID card for use at military medical facilities;
Leave and Earnings Statement showing Family Separation Allowance or allotments to

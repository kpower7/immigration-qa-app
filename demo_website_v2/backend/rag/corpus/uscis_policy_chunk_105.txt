7 USCIS-PM A.3 - Chapter 3 - Filing Instructions
Technical Update - Naturalization for Surviving Spouse, Child, or Parent of
Service Member
December 18, 2019
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is clarifying guidance in the USCIS Policy Manual
to indicate that the spouse, child, or parent of a deceased U.S. citizen member of the U.S. armed
forces who died “during a period of honorable service” (instead of as the result of honorable service)
may be eligible for naturalization as the surviving relative of the service member , consistent with the
statutory language in INA  319(d).

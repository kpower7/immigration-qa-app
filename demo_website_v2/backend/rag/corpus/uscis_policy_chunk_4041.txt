Chapter 6 - Jurisdiction, Place of Residence, and Early Filing
A. Three-Month Residency Requirement (in State or Service District)
In general, an applicant for naturalization must file his or her application for naturalization with the
state or service district that has jurisdiction over his or her place of residence. The applicant must have
resided in that location for at least three months prior to filing. 
The term “state” includes the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, Guam, the V irgin Islands of the United
States, and the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands (CNMI).[1] The term “service district”
is defined as the geographical area over which a USCIS of fice has jurisdiction.[2]

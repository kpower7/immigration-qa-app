POLICY  ALER T - O-1 Nonimmigrant Status for Persons of Extraordinary Ability
July 22, 2022
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is updating policy guidance in the USCIS Policy
Manual to further clarify how USCIS evaluates evidence to determine eligibility for O-1A
nonimmigrants of extraordinary ability , with a focus on persons in science, technology , engineering, or
mathematics (STEM) fields.

unlawful presence ground.[7] Whether a specific inadmissibility ground applies to an alien depends on
an analysis of the facts of the alien’ s case in light of that specific ground.
It is possible that an alien’ s immigration history makes the alien inadmissible under both the 3-year or
10-year unlawful presence grounds of inadmissibility and the permanent unlawful presence ground of
inadmissibility . Additionally , an alien who accrued the requisite unlawful presence and who is removed
from the United States may be inadmissible as an alien previously removed under INA 212(a)(9)(A)[8]
and under the unlawful presence grounds, depending on the circumstances of the alien’ s case.

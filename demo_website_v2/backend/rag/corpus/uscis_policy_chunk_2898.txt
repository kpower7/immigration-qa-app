A. Purpose and Background[1]
Congress created the special immigrant juvenile (SIJ) classification when it enacted the Immigration
Act of 1990 (IMMACT  90).[2] Certain juveniles in the United States may be eligible for SIJ
classification. Once classified as an SIJ, juveniles may be eligible to adjust status, if they meet all
eligibility requirements.

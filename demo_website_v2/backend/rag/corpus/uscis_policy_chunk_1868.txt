C. Schedule A Occupations
For certain occupations, DOL  has predetermined that there are not suf ficient U.S. workers who are
able, willing, qualified, and available. These occupations are referred to as Schedule A occupations,
and the process to satisfy the permanent labor certification requirement is referred to as “blanket”
labor certification. DOL  has predetermined that the wages and working conditions of U.S. workers
similarly employed will not be adversely af fected by the employment of aliens in those occupations.

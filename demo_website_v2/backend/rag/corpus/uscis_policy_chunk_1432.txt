B. Orphan
1. Applicability
The petitioner may follow the orphan process if:
At least one of the P APs is a U.S. citizen; and
The child is not from a Hague Adoption Convention country .[19]
If the child is from a Hague Adoption Convention country , a U.S. citizen cannot pursue the orphan
petition process unless the petitioner establishes that the Hague Adoption Convention does not apply .
[20]
2. Process
For the orphan process, the petitioner must file a suitability application[21] and a petition[22] for the
child. The following table provides a summary of requirements to obtain lawful immigration status in
the United States for the child through the orphan process.

4. Treatment of Family Members
The spouse or child (unmarried and under 21 years of age) of a special immigrant broadcaster may , if
otherwise eligible, accompany or follow-to-join the principal applicant.[20] The spouse and child may ,
as derivative applicants, apply to adjust status under the same immigrant category and priority date as
the principal applicant.

L-1 Beneficiary May Own the Organization
The beneficiary may own the foreign or U.S. organization in whole or in part. However , maintaining a
ceremonial title and position, such as Director or President, without being primarily engaged in the
management of the organization, is not qualifying for L-1 purposes.[7]

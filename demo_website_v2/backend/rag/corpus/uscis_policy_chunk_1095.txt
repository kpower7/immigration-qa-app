The applicant may document extreme hardship through a personal statement or other evidence,
including evidence from relevant country condition reports and any other public or private sources of
information. The applicant may include evidence of hardship arising from circumstances surrounding
the victimization and any other circumstances.[63] USCIS does not consider evidence of hardship to
persons other than the applicant unless the evidence specifically demonstrates hardship to the
applicant as a result of hardship to another person.
Applicants under the age of 18 are not exempt from the extreme hardship requirement. However ,
USCIS considers an applicant’ s age, maturity , and personal circumstances (among other factors)
when evaluating the extreme hardship requirement.[64]

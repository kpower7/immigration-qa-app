Chapter 2 - Replacement of Permanent Resident Card
A. Eligibility Requirements
Lawful permanent residents (LPRs) are entitled to evidence of status in the United States. LPRs are
eligible for replacement of their Permanent Resident Card (PRC) if they meet requirements, including
but not limited to, the following:
Properly file the Application to Replace Permanent Resident Card ( Form I-90 );
Establish identity;
Establish LPR or conditional permanent resident (CPR)[1] status; and
Otherwise meet the eligibility requirements to receive a replacement PRC.
Maintaining LPR or CPR Status
LPR status generally begins from the date the government admits an alien to the United States as an
LPR or grants or recognizes LPR status. LPR status ends if and when rescinded by USCIS,[2]

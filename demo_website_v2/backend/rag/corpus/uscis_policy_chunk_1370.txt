Chapter 2 - Eligibility Requirements
The Immigration and Nationality Act (INA) provides for the admission of alien spouses and children
(derivatives) of asylees, refugees, and lawful permanent residents (LPRs) who received this status as
a principal refugee or principal asylee.[1]
A. General Eligibility Requirements
An eligible petitioner may petition for their spouse and children by filing a Refugee/Asylee Relative
Petition ( Form I-730 ). The petitioner must file a separate Form I-730 for each qualifying family
member .[2]

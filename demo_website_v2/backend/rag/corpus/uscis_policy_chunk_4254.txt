A child born outside the United States acquires U.S. citizenship at birth under INA  301 if at the time of
the child’ s birth:
The person is a child[8] of a U.S. citizen parent(s);
The child’ s legal parents are married to each other and at least one legal parent is the genetic or
gestational parent of that child at the time of the child’ s birth; and
The parent meets the residence or physical presence requirements under the applicable law and
the child meets all other applicable requirements under INA 301.

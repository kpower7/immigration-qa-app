A principal applicant may request USCIS to reconsider the denial by filing a Notice of Appeal or Motion
(Form I-290B ). Additionally , both principal applicants and derivatives may appeal a denial of an
application for T nonimmigrant status to the Administrative Appeals Of fice (AAO) using the Form I-

Chapter 2 - Signatures
A. Signature Requirement
USCIS requires a valid signature on applications, petitions, requests, and certain other documents
filed with USCIS.[1] Except as otherwise specifically authorized, a benefit requestor must personally
sign his or her own request before filing it with USCIS.[2]
In order to maintain the integrity of the immigration benefit system and validate the identity of benefit
requestors, USCIS rejects any benefit request with an improper signature and returns it to the
requestor .[3] USCIS does not provide an opportunity to correct (or cure) a deficient signature. The

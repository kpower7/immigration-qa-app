supplemental evidence at the time of the request or in response to an RFE.
When there are multiple filing bases, USCIS only approves a waiver based on one category . If the
CPR establishes eligibility for the battery or extreme cruelty waiver , USCIS approves on this basis
before any other bases the CPR may establish. Of ficers must adjudicate waivers using any credible
evidence, and the confidentiality protections of 8 U.S.C. 1367  attach to the entire petition in any case
involving a waiver based on battery or extreme cruelty .

C. Deportability Based on Events Arising After Adjustment
If a person becomes removable as a result of an event that occurred after adjustment of status to
lawful permanent residence, he or she is not subject to rescission as a result of the event. However ,
the of ficer may refer the case for possible initiation of removal proceedings if the person is deemed
removable.[13]
D. Rescission of Adjustment After Naturalization
In general, naturalization is revoked before the rescission of adjustment of status.[14]

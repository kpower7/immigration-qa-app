4. Treatment of Family Members
The spouse or child (unmarried and under 21 years of age) of a special immigrant physician, if
otherwise eligible, may accompany or follow-to-join the principal applicant.[15] As derivative applicants,
the spouse and child may apply to adjust status under the same immigrant category and priority date
as the principal applicant.

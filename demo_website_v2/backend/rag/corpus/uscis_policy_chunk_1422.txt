The Displaced Persons Act of 1948 contained the first laws relating to the immigration of foreign-born
orphans. In enacting legislation on foreign-born orphans and adoptees, Congress was primarily
concerned with the welfare of the children. Since then, Congress has enacted several acts and
amendments related to orphans and intercountry adoption. The table below provides an overview of
major legislation and international treaties related to intercountry adoption.

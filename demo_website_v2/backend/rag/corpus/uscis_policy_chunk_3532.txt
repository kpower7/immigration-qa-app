As the table illustrates, a fraud finding encompasses a willful misrepresentation finding. Therefore, if
all the elements are present to make a finding of fraud, then the elements for making a finding of willful
misrepresentation must also necessarily be present. 
Example:
The of ficer finds that an alien obtained an immigration benefit by fraud. The alien is then inadmissible
for both fraud[12] and willful misrepresentation.
Example: 
The of ficer finds that there was no intent to deceive, but the other elements of fraud are present. The
alien is not inadmissible based on fraud but is still inadmissible for willful misrepresentation.[13]

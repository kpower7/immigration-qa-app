Q. Review , Signature, and Attestation
1. Requirements
The home study preparer must sign the home study , include a declaration, and date the home study
(and any updates). The home study preparer ’s signature on the home study (and any updates) must
be original.
An accredited agency must review and approve the home study if not completed in the first instance
by an accredited agency , unless a public domestic or public foreign authority conducted the home
study .[81] Therefore, if an approved person, supervised provider , or exempted provider conducted the
home study , an accredited agency must review and approve the home study before the P AP submits it
to USCIS.[82]
If the P AP resides in a state that requires the state’ s (child welfare) authorities to review the home

USCIS may temporarily or permanently suspend or debar a new commercial enterprise or job-creating
entity , or any individual or business entity associated with those entities, for specified statutory
violations, such as knowingly involving an individual or entity who does not meet the statutory bona
fides requirements, by failing to take commercially reasonable ef forts to discontinue the prohibited
individual or entity's involvement, or provide notice to USCIS within 14 days of acquiring such
knowledge.[47]
Additional information on the process for issuing sanctions appears in Chapter 8, Sanctions and
Discretionary Determinations [ 6 USCIS-PM G.8 ].

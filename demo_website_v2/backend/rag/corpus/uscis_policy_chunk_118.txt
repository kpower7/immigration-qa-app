Technical Update - Certificates of Citizenship for U.S. National Children
August 15, 2018
This technical update clarifies that a person who is born a U.S. national and is the child of a U.S.
citizen may acquire citizenship and may obtain a Certificate of Citizenship without having to establish
lawful permanent resident status.

Part B - 245(a) Adjustment
Chapter 1 - Purpose and Background
A. Purpose
Section 245 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA) allows certain aliens who are physically
present in the United States to adjust status to that of a lawful permanent resident (LPR). Most
adjustment applicants file their adjustment of status applications based on INA 245(a) .

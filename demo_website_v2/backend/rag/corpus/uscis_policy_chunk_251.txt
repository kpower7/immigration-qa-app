3. Retaliation
USCIS does not tolerate retaliation against any person for reporting discrimination. This includes filing
a complaint, helping any other person file a complaint, or participating in an inquiry into potential
violations of this policy . Any employee found to have engaged in retaliatory conduct or behavior is
subject to disciplinary action.
E. Reporting Fraud, Abuse, and Scams
Benefit requestors and other interested parties should report fraud, abuse, and scams as indicated on
the USCIS Contact Us  page.
In addition, immigration fraud can be reported to:
Immigration and Customs Enforcement ;
Department of Labor ’s Wage and Hour Division ;
The Federal Trade Commission ; and
State authorities .
The USCIS website also contains information on common scams  and how to avoid scams .

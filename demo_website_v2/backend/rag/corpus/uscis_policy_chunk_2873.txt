Chapter 5 - Certain Physicians
A. Purpose and Background
Prior to 1981, the United States required graduates of foreign medical schools licensed and
practicing medicine in the United States to have passed the V isa Qualifying Examination (VQE) in
order to immigrate as a practicing (clinical) physician. To address an immediate need for physicians
and avoid bureaucratic delays, Congress eliminated this requirement in 1981[1] for certain foreign
medical graduate physicians as part of a new special immigrant category .[2] This program has no
sunset date.

INA 203(b)(4)  – Certain special immigrants

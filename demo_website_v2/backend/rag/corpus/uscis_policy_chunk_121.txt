12 USCIS-PM J.2  - Chapter 2 - The Oath of Allegiance
Technical Update - Military Accessions V ital to National Interest
March 21, 2018
This technical update clarifies that foreign nationals may apply for military naturalization after the
certification of honorable service has been properly processed by the U.S. armed forces.

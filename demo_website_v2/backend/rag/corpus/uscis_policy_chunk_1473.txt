Like other criminal history results, police clearance letters are also valid for a period of 15 months from

6. Home Study[15]
A PAP must submit a valid, original[16] home study to USCIS with the suitability application.[17]
The P AP may need to update the home study during the intercountry adoption process.[18] The home
study , or the most recent update of the home study , cannot be more than 6 months old at the time of
submission to USCIS.[19]
This means that no more than 6 months have passed since the home study preparer signed and
dated the home study . If a home study will be more than 6 months old at the time of submission to
USCIS, the P AP must ensure that it is updated by the home study preparer before submission.[20]

determination of admissibility has been made. The alien continues to have refugee status until such
time that the alien is inspected and an admissibility determination is made.
The of ficer should write a denial notice explaining the reasons for denial in clear language that the
alien can understand. There is no appeal from the denial, but the alien may renew the application for
adjustment while in removal proceedings before the immigration judge.[9]

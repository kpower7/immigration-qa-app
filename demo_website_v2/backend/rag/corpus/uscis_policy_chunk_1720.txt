Documentation that the child has been jointly residing with the adoptive parent(s) for at least 2
years (at the time of filing).
In order to be issued a Certificate of Citizenship, if the child is over the age of 14, the child must take
the Oath of Allegiance, unless otherwise waived, before the age of 18.[39]

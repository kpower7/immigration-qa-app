6. Effect of Exemption
An applicant who qualifies under INA 245(k)  is exempt from the INA 245(c)(2) , INA 245(c)(7) , and INA
245(c)(8)  adjustment bars. This does not, however , exempt an applicant from any
other bar , eligibility requirement, or ground of inadmissibility .[17] Therefore, once an of ficer determines
that the applicant qualifies for the exemption, the of ficer must next determine whether
the applicant is otherwise eligible for adjustment.

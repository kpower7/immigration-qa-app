D. Parts of Form I-693 Addressing Physical or Mental Disorders
The civil surgeon must check the appropriate findings box on the medical examination report. The civil
surgeon should also either annotate the findings in the remarks section or attach a report, if the space
provided is not suf ficient. However , the of ficer should not RFE simply because the civil surgeon has
omitted the remarks or failed to attach a report.

Chapter 3 - Expired Permanent Resident Cards
To deter fraud and enhance security , USCIS field of fices generally collect expired Permanent Resident
Cards (PRCs) encountered through the normal course of business, unless the PRC has an unexpired
extension sticker or a valid receipt notice to be used with the expired PRC as evidence of continuing
LPR status.
Offices that have collected expired cards should follow agency procedures to update applicable

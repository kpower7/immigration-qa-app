Change of Year for T ax Purposes
An organization cannot change its year for tax purposes (for example, from a fiscal year to a calendar
year) without permission from the IRS. Tax returns for consecutive years that have dif ferent reporting
years may be an indication that the documents are fraudulent. In addition, the ending balances on the
balance sheet for 1 year should match the beginning balances for the next year .

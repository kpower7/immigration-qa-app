Chapter 2 - Determining False Claim to U.S. Citizenship
For an alien to be inadmissible based on false claim to U.S. citizenship, an of ficer must find all of the
following elements:
The alien made a representation of U.S. citizenship;
The representation was false;[1] and
The alien made the false representation for any purpose or benefit under the Immigration and

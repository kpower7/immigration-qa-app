Only orphans or Convention adoptees whose adoptive or prospective adoptive parents have signed
an af fidavit will be exempt from the vaccination requirement. If the adopting parent(s) prefers that the
child meet the vaccination requirement as part of the visa application process, the child may benefit
from the waiver(s) for those vaccinations which the panel physician determines are medically
inappropriate.[22]
When the adoptive or prospective adoptive parent cannot sign the af fidavit in good faith because of
religious or moral objections to vaccinations, the child will require a waiver .[23]

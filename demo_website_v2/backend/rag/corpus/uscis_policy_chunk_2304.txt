A. Number of V isas

“Special Immigrant Status for Persons Serving as Translators with United States Armed Forces,”
authorized the issuance of up to 50 special immigrant visas per fiscal year to Iraqi and Afghan
translators and interpreters working for the U.S. government.[1] Congress increased the total number

Chapter 2 - Eligibility
A person who has adjusted status to that of a person lawfully admitted for permanent residence under
INA 210, 240A , (the former) 244, 245, 245A , or 249, or under any other provision of law may be
placed into rescission proceedings at any time during the first 5 years after the granting of permanent
residence, if:
USCIS determines that the person was not eligible for adjustment of status at the time that
permanent residence was granted; and
The person would have not been eligible for adjustment under any other provision of law .[1]
A determination that a person is not subject to rescission proceedings does not necessarily mean that
no further action may be taken. A person who is not subject to rescission may still be subject to
removal proceedings.[2]

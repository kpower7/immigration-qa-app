An applicant is not required to take the Oath of Allegiance or appear at the oath ceremony if USCIS
waives the oath requirement due to the applicant’ s medical disability . In these cases, USCIS issues
the certificate in person or by certified mail to the person or his or her legal guardian, surrogate, or
designated representative.[13]

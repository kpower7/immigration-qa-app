Applicants may appeal USCIS’  decision to deny an application for a waiver of inadmissibility sought in

D. Revocation of W aivers
USCIS, at any time, may revoke a waiver previously granted as part of the application for T
nonimmigrant status or adjustment of status under INA 212(a)  or INA 212(d) .[7] There is no appeal of a
decision to revoke a waiver .[8]

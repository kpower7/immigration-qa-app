charge bond, USCIS must invite the alien to do so.[36] No public charge bonds will be accepted
without an invitation from USCIS.
The public charge bond is posted on the condition that the applicant does not become a public charge
after adjusting status.[37] If the applicant does not maintain all conditions of the public charge bond,
the bond is breached, and the U.S. government will demand payment on the bond.
If the applicant does not become a public charge while the bond is in ef fect, then USCIS will cancel
the bond upon the request from either the obligor or agent or co-obligor (the one who posted the
bond), or the applicant.[38]

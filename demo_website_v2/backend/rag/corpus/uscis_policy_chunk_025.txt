POLICY  ALER T - Customer Service and Interpretation of 8 U.S.C. 1367
Confidentiality Protections for U.S. Citizens
June 12, 2024
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is issuing guidance in the USCIS Policy Manual
that interprets the confidentiality protections under 8 U.S.C. 1367 as ending at naturalization. USCIS is
making this change as the previous practice of maintaining 8 U.S.C. 1367 protections beyond
naturalization created burdens for some naturalized U.S. citizens. This policy will result in naturalized
citizens having full access to USCIS electronic benefit processing and critical customer service tools
that are available to other U.S. citizens. This guidance becomes ef fective July 12, 2024.

Denial
If the of ficer determines that the applicant is ineligible for adjustment, the of ficer must deny the
adjustment application. The of ficer must provide the applicant a written reason for the denial.[33]
Although there are no appeal rights for the denial of an adjustment application, the applicant may file a
motion to reopen or reconsider . The denial notice should include instructions for filing a Notice of
Appeal or Motion ( Form I-290B ).

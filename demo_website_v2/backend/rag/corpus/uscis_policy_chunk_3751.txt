4. Exemption from Public Charge Ground of Inadmissibility
There is a statutory exemption from the public charge ground of inadmissibility[12] for victims who
apply for T nonimmigrant status.[13] This exemption also applies to T nonimmigrant adjustment of
status applicants.[14] If, however , a T nonimmigrant is applying for adjustment of status based on an
employment-based petition that requires an Affidavit of Support Under Section 213A  of the INA  (Form
I-864 ), the applicant must submit an af fidavit of support as described in INA 212(a)(4)(D) .[15]

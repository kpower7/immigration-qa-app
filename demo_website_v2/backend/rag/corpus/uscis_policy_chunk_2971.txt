The table below is an example of the cut-of f number system. Only selectees with visas available and

The V isa Bulletin for March 2019 states that the rank number cut-of f for visa allocation in March for the
Europe region is 13,500.[56] This means that as of March 2019, visas were available only for
selectees in the Europe region with rank numbers 1 through 13,499.

USCIS imbeds a photograph when creating secure documents as a security feature.[28] There are
instances where USCIS requires a photograph be submitted with an application, petition, or request in
order to create a secure document and the application, petition, or request does not have an
associated biometrics collection requirement.[29] Where the applicant, petitioner , or requestor fails to
submit a photograph at time of filing, USCIS may issue a Request for Evidence.

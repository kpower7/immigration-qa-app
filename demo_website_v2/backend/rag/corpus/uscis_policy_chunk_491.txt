C. Split Decisions in Extension Requests
Officers may , when warranted, deny an applicant or petitioner ’s request to extend the nonimmigrant’ s
stay in the United States in the same classification.[16] Even if an applicant or petitioner continues to
demonstrate eligibility for the nonimmigrant classification, an of ficer may determine that suf ficient
reason exists to deny the request for an extension of stay (such as inadmissibility factors or failure to
maintain status).
This “split” decision process may result in approval of the petition for the same classification where the
petitioner and the beneficiary relationship has not changed, and a simultaneous denial of the
extension of stay request.

D. Application for Certificate of Citizenship (Form N-600)
1. Submission of Application
A person born outside the United States who acquires U.S. citizenship at birth is not required to have
evidence of such status. However , if the person seeks documentation of U.S. citizenship status, the
person may file an Application for Certificate of Citizenship ( Form N-600 ) with USCIS.[48]

I. Suspected Fraud
If USCIS determines that the marriage was designed to circumvent U.S. immigration laws, then
USCIS issues an RFE or NOID and gives the CPR an opportunity to rebut the evidence of record.[48]
If the CPR files a waiver or individual filing request, USCIS provides this opportunity only to the CPR.
USCIS examines the CPR’ s response to the RFE or NOID. If the response is insuf ficient to overcome
the derogatory information in the record, and USCIS still finds that despite the rebuttal the marriage
was designed to circumvent the immigration laws, then USCIS denies the case.

POLICY  ALER T - EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program Modernization Final Rule
November 06, 2019
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is revising its policy guidance in the USCIS Policy
Manual to align with the EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program Modernization Final Rule, published on July
24, 2019, and ef fective November 21, 2019. Note: On June 22, 2021, the U.S. District Court for the
Northern District of California, in Behring Regional Center LLC v . Wolf, 20-cv-09263-JSC,
vacated the EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program Modernization Final Rule (PDF) . While USCIS
considers this decision, USCIS will apply the EB-5 regulations and policies that were in effect
before the rule was finalized on November 21, 2019.

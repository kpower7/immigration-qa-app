any unlawful presence that may have accrued between the original denial and the new decision. The
result is that the applicant will not have accrued any unlawful presence from the original filing of the
adjustment application until there is a final decision. 
If the applicant is otherwise inadmissible because of unlawful presence accrued before applying for
adjustment, the applicant must seek a waiver or other form of relief to address the inadmissibility .[33]

Evidence of Applicant’ s Intent to Reside Abroad with Citizen Spouse and Return to the United States
Upon T ermination of Qualifying Employment
Along with his or her naturalization application, an applicant for naturalization under INA  319(b) must
submit a statement describing his or her intent to reside abroad with the citizen spouse and his or her
intent to take up residence within the United States immediately upon the termination of such
employment abroad of the citizen spouse.[26]

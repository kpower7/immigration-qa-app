Before DOS issues any immigrant visa and the child immigrates to the United States.
Both DHS and DOS have rules that govern home studies for intercountry adoptions. The Intercountry
Adoption Universal Accreditation Act of 2012 (UAA),[5] effective July 14, 2014, impacted requirements
for home studies in orphan cases, including home study preparation, home study elements, and the
duty of disclosure.[6] As a result and unless an exception applies,[7] Hague Adoption Convention and
orphan suitability determinations are now very similar .

E. Adjudication[26]
1. Filing
An applicant seeking adjustment of status as a special immigrant broadcaster may file his or her
adjustment application with USCIS after USCIS approves the special immigrant petition[27] (as long as
the petition is still valid), provided:
USCIS has jurisdiction over the adjustment application;[28] and
 The visa availability requirements are met.[29]
These applicants may not file an adjustment application concurrently with Form I-360 .

Chapter 3 - Certificate of Naturalization
A. Eligibility for Certificate of Naturalization
An applicant submits to USCIS an Application for Naturalization ( Form N-400 ) along with supporting
evidence to establish eligibility for naturalization. The application must be submitted in accordance
with the form instructions and with appropriate fee.[1] The applicant must establish that he or she has
met all of the pertinent naturalization eligibility requirements for issuance of a Certificate of
Naturalization.[2]
B. Contents of Certificate of Naturalization
1. Information about the Applicant
The Certificate of Naturalization contains certain required information identifying the person and
confirming the person's U.S. citizenship through naturalization. Specifically , the Certificate of
Naturalization contains:
USCIS registration number (A-number);
Complete name;
Marital status;

POLICY  ALER T - Customer Contact Options for Certain Asylum-Related and
Other Protected Populations
January 07, 2025
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is updating policy guidance in the USCIS Policy
Manual to clarify customer service options available to certain noncitizens whose applications or
processes are subject to confidentiality protections. Such noncitizens include applicants of the
Application for Asylum and for Withholding of Removal ( Form I-589 ); applicants of the Application for
Suspension of Deportation or Special Rule Cancellation of Removal (Pursuant to Section 203 of
Public Law 105-100 (NACARA)) ( Form I-881 ); and noncitizens in the credible fear , reasonable fear ,
and Safe Third Country Agreement screening processes.

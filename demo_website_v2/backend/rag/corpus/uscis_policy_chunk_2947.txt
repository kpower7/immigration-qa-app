3. Admissibility and W aiver Requirements
In general, an applicant who is inadmissible to the United States may only obtain LPR status if the
applicant obtains a waiver or other form of relief, if available.[22] In general, if a ground of
inadmissibility applies, an applicant must apply for a waiver or other form of relief to overcome that
inadmissibility .[23] If a waiver or other form of relief is granted, USCIS may approve the application to
adjust status if the applicant is otherwise eligible.

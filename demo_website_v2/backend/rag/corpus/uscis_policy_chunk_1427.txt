Chapter 2 - Adoption Processes
The primary elements that determine which adoption process (Hague Adoption Convention, orphan, or
family-based) an adoptive parent (petitioner) may follow include:
The child’ s country of habitual residence;
The petitioner ’s citizenship and country of habitual residence;
The date of any adoption or legal custody order; and
The length of time the child has been in the legal custody of and jointly resided with the
petitioner .

Chapter 3 - Fees
Requestors must include any required fees with the submission of a benefit request to USCIS. This
payment must be in U.S. currency .[1]
The fee amount for each benefit request is controlled by regulation[2] and is published in the Fee
Schedule ( Form G-1055 ). The total fee amount for each request is not determined solely by the fee
required for the associated form.[3] Additional fees may be required, such as a biometric services
fee[4] or the fraud detection and prevention fee.[5]
The additional services needed in a given situation dictate which additional fees, if any , are added to

B. Overview of Discretion
1. Definition
The Board of Immigration Appeals (BIA) has described the exercise of discretion as:
A balancing of the negative factors evidencing the person’ s undesirability as a permanent
resident with the social and humane considerations presented on his or her behalf to determine
whether relief appears in the best interests of this country .[31]8/13/25, 3:54 AM Policy Manual | USCIS

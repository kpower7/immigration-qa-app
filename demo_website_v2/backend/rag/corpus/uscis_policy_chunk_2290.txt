Deceased Principal Alien (Pending or Approved Form I-360)
Where the principal alien had a pending or an approved Form I-360 petition, the eligibility of the
surviving spouse and children is also af fected by INA 204(l) ,[17] since the surviving spouse and
children are derivative beneficiaries of an employment-based immigrant visa petition.[18] In light of the
interrelationship between Section 1244 of NDAA  2008 and INA  204(l):
USCIS may approve a pending alien visa petition under Section 1244 despite the death of the
principal immigrant while the petition is pending; or
After the death of the principal alien, USCIS may favorably exercise discretion to reinstate the
approval of a visa petition under Section 1244.

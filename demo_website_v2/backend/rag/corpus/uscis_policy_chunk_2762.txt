4. Effect of Grandfathering
Once a 245(i) adjustment applicant establishes that he or she is a grandfathered alien, the applicant
remains grandfathered and future eligibility for adjustment under INA 245(i)  is preserved until the
applicant adjusts to LPR status.[39] The applicant may use the qualifying petition or application as the
basis for adjustment of status if the petition or application is still valid. In addition, the applicant may
seek to adjust under another family-based, employment-based, special immigrant, or diversity visa
immigrant category for which the applicant is eligible.[40]

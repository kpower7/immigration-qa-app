Juvenile Delinquency
Findings of juvenile delinquency are not considered criminal convictions for purposes of immigration
law. However , certain grounds of inadmissibility do not require a conviction. In some cases, certain
conduct alone may be suf ficient to trigger an inadmissibility ground.[32]

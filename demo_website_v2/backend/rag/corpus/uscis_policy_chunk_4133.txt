Chapter 5 - Conditional Bars for Acts in Statutory Period
In addition to the permanent bars to good moral character (GMC), the Immigration and Nationality Act
(INA) and corresponding regulations include bars to GMC that are not permanent in nature. USCIS
refers to these bars as “conditional bars.” These bars are triggered by specific
acts, of fenses, activities, circumstances, or convictions within the statutory period for naturalization,
including the period prior to filing and up to the time of the Oath of Allegiance.[1] An of fense that does
not fall within a permanent or conditional bar to GMC may nonetheless af fect an applicant’ s ability
to establish GMC.[2]
With regard to bars to GMC requiring a conviction, the of ficer reviews the relevant federal or state law
or regulation of the United States, or law or regulation of any foreign country to determine whether the
applicant can establish GMC.

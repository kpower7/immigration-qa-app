In addition, a non-genetic, non-gestational legal parent may file a Form I-130  on behalf of the child if
the parent is married to the child’ s genetic or gestational parent at the time of the child’ s birth and both
parents are recognized by the relevant jurisdiction as the child’ s legal parents. Under those
circumstances, the child is considered born in wedlock.[11]
Legal parentage is generally determined under the laws of the jurisdiction in which the child was born,
but there may be circumstances in which the law of the jurisdiction of residence applies, such as when
a post-partum act of legitimation occurs in the jurisdiction of residence.

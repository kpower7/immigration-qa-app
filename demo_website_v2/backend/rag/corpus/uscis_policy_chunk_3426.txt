C. Applicants Not Required to File Form I-864
Certain applicants are not statutorily required to submit a Form I-864. Other applicants, although
generally required to file a Form I-864, may be exempt from this requirement. Being exempt from the
Form I-864 requirement is dif ferent from being exempt from the public charge inadmissibility ground.
[36]
An of ficer still makes an inadmissibility determination for an applicant who is exempt from the Form I-
864 filing requirement unless the alien is also exempt from the public charge ground of inadmissibility .

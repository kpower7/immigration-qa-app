3. Program Evolution
Congress initially authorized the Regional Center Program as a trial pilot program, set to expire after 5
years. Congress extended the Regional Center Program several times before codifying the Regional
Center Program into INA 203(b)(5)  as part of the EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act of 2022, which
became ef fective on May 14, 2022 and authorizes the Regional Center Program through September
30, 2027.[18] The EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act of 2022 also included other substantive revisions to
the EB-5 program more generally .[19]

accredited by the Board of Immigration Appeals. In general, adoption agencies do not qualify as
accredited representatives. 
Information about the particulars of any case can only be given to the P AP, or to an attorney or
accredited representative with a Form G-28 or Form G-28I on file. The Privacy Act forbids disclosing
information about the case to anyone else, unless the P AP has signed a written consent[51] to the
disclosure or such disclosure is not prohibited by law .[52]

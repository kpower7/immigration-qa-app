Suitability Application Exception
USCIS deems the suitability application filing date to be the petition filing date if both of the following
requirements are met:[7]
The P AP filed the suitability application after the child's 15th birthday , but before the child's 16th
birthday (or , if the sibling exception also applies, after the child's 17th birthday , but before the
child's 18th birthday); and
The P AP files the petition not more than 180 days after the initial approval of the suitability
application.

The T-1 principal may file the Application for Derivative T Nonimmigrant Status ( Form I-914,
Supplement A) at the following times:
Concurrently with the principal’ s Application for T Nonimmigrant Status ( Form I-914 );
While the principal’ s application is pending; or
At any time after the principal’ s application is approved, so long as the principal continues to hold
T-1 nonimmigrant status.[12]
If the principal no longer holds T-1 nonimmigrant status because the validity period has expired and
has not been extended, USCIS denies the application for the eligible family member .

Chapter 6 - Required Background Checks
USCIS conducts security and background checks on all applicants for naturalization. Members or
former members of the U.S. armed forces applying for naturalization must comply with those
requirements. This chapter provides information on specific background checks required of such
applicants. This chapter also provides information on the ways service members may meet the
fingerprint requirement for naturalization.

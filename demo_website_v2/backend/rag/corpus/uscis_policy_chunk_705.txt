Aliens who meet the above definition may be eligible for classification as a foreign information media
representative. Foreign information media representative nonimmigrants are admitted for the duration
of their employment with the same foreign media organization in the same information medium.
Foreign information media representatives must obtain authorization from USCIS to change
employers or work in a dif ferent medium.[2]
Independent Production Companies[3]
Employees of independent production companies may also be eligible for foreign information media
representative nonimmigrant status if, in addition to the above:
The employee holds a credential issued by a professional journalistic association; 
The film or video footage produced will be used by a foreign-based television station or other
media to disseminate information or news to a foreign audience; and 
The film or video footage will not be used primarily for a commercial entertainment or advertising
purpose.

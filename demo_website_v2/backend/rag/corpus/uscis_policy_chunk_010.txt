Technical Update - Replacing the Term "Noncitizen"
February 26, 2025
This technical update replaces all instances of the term “noncitizen” with “alien” or other appropriate
terms throughout the Policy Manual where possible, as used to refer to a person who meets the
definition provided in INA  101(a)(3) [“any person not a citizen or national of the United States”], and
makes other technical and conforming changes.

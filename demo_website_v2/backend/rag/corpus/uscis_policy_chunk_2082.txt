Sheepherders
A sheepherder is an unskilled worker . An alien sheepherder who has been legally employed as a
nonimmigrant sheepherder in the United States for at least 33 of the preceding 36 months is not
required to obtain an approved permanent labor certification from the U.S. Department of Labor .
Instead, the petitioner files the permanent labor certification application directly with the appropriate
USCIS of fice or the U.S. Department of State. This procedure relates only to the permanent labor
certification process and has no bearing on the amount of training or experience needed to perform
the job.

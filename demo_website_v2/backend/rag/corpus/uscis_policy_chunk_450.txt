C. Action on an Approved Application or Petition
In most instances, once a benefit request is adjudicated and notices are sent to the relevant parties,
no further action on the part of the of ficer is required. However , there are certain situations that may
require additional actions. Such actions may be initiated by the requestor , ordinarily by filing an
Application for Action on an Approved Application or Petition ( Form I-824 ).
For example, Form I-824 may be filed, with fee, to request a duplicate approval notice or to transfer a
visa petition requiring visa issuance from one consulate to another .
Jurisdiction to act on a Form I-824 lies with the of fice that originally approved the underlying benefit or ,
if the file has been transferred, with the of fice currently holding the file. Of ficers should follow local

Principal and derivative T nonimmigrant applicants for adjustment of status who are inadmissible
based on a pre-existing ground not previously waived in connection with the application for T

The applicant should submit any additional evidence that is relevant to the exercise of discretion.[3]
C. W aivers for Grounds of Inadmissibility
USCIS has the authority under INA 245(l)(2)  to waive any ground of inadmissibility for T nonimmigrant
applicants for adjustment of status, with the exception of the following:
Security and related grounds - INA 212(a)(3) ;
International child abduction - INA 212(a)(10)(C) ; and
Renunciation of U.S. citizenship to avoid taxation - INA 212(a)(10)(E) .[4]
T nonimmigrant applicants for adjustment of status may apply for any other waivers of inadmissibility
for which they may be eligible, in addition to INA 245(l)(2) .
1. Health-Related Inadmissibility Grounds
The standard for waiving health-related[5] inadmissibility grounds is whether a waiver is in the national
interest as a matter of discretion.[6]
2. Other Inadmissibility Grounds
For other grounds of inadmissibility , the standard is whether:
It is in the national interest to waive the inadmissibility ground; and
The activities rendering the applicant inadmissible were caused by , or were incident to, the T-1
principal applicant’ s traf ficking victimization.[7]

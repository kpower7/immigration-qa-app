3. Termination and Revocation
USCIS may terminate the grant of deferred action and revoke the BFD EAD[23] at any time if it
determines the favorable exercise of discretion or BFD EAD are no longer warranted, or the prior
deferred action and BFD EAD were granted in error . For example, USCIS may terminate deferred
action and revoke the BFD EAD if USCIS identifies any adverse information, such as new information
pertaining to the risks the applicant poses to national security or public safety , or any other relevant
adverse discretionary factor .

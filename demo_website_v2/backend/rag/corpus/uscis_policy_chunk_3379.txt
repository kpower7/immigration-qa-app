Chapter 2 - Definitions
Under the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA) 212(a)(4) , an applicant for adjustment of status is
inadmissible if, in the opinion of the immigration of ficer at the time of application for adjustment of
status, they are “likely at any time to become a public charge.”[1]
A. Likely at Any T ime to Become a Public Charge
The INA  does not define the phrase “likely at any time to become a public charge.”[2]
The regulations at 8 CFR 212.21(a)  define “likely at any time to become a public charge” to mean
“likely at any time to become primarily dependent on the government for subsistence, as
demonstrated by either the receipt of public cash assistance for income maintenance or long-term
institutionalization at government expense.”[3]

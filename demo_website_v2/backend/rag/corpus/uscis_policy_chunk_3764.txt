[35] The Form I-94 is not evidence of identity and for the categories of aliens listed above can be

To obtain a document evidencing both identity and employment authorization, these aliens may apply
for an EAD, with the appropriate fee (or request a fee waiver), if applicable. However , certain
categories of aliens are automatically issued an EAD showing both identity and employment
authorization based on their status and are not required to apply separately for an EAD.[36]

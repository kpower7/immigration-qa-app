If the Central Authority of that country determines the P AP is habitually resident in the country where
the P AP resides, and it is the receiving country for the purposes of adoption of a child from another
Hague Adoption Convention partner country , the P AP generally must follow the Hague Adoption
Convention process of the country where the P AP is habitually resident.
The P AP may also be able to simultaneously pursue the U.S. Hague Adoption Convention process to
immigrate the child to the United States if the P AP is able to obtain the required Convention
documents from the Central Authority in the child’ s country of origin. However , if the P AP is not able to
obtain the required Convention documents, the U.S. Hague Adoption Convention process may not be
available.

D. Role of the Department of Homeland Security (DHS)
Congress authorizes the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) to determine an alien’ s admissibility
to the United States, which includes determinations based on health reasons.[7] DHS must follow HHS
regulations and instructions when determining whether an applicant is inadmissible on health-related
grounds.[8]
Congress also empowers DHS to designate qualified physicians as civil surgeons who conduct
medical examinations of aliens physically present in the United States.[9]

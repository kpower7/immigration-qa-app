Part H - Designated and Special Immigrants
Chapter 1 - Purpose and Background
A. Purpose
A special immigrant is an alien who may qualify for lawful permanent residence under certain
provisions of the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA). Special immigrants are eligible to apply for
lawful admission as a permanent resident or adjustment of status to permanent residence.

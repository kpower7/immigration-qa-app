by-case basis to grant BFD Employment Authorization Documents (BFD EADs) and deferred action.
The BFD process is distinct from the waiting list process. Before June 2021, a principal petitioner and
his or her qualifying family members received employment authorization and deferred action only
when USCIS placed the principal petitioner on the waiting list.

General Guidelines for Adjudication of Orphan Petition
Ensure the prospective adoptive parent (P AP) meets suitability determination and eligibility
requirements.[1]
Verify the child’ s identity .
Determine if the child meets age requirements.
Determine if any of the terms in the orphan definition apply to the child.[2]
Determine if there is evidence of a “full and final" adoption or legal custody for emigration and
adoption in the United States.[3]
Verify there is no material evidence of fraud, child-buying, other misrepresentation, or non-
bona fide intent that impacts eligibility .
Confirm the child matches the characteristics that the P AP has been approved to adopt.
Verify the petitioner has identified an accredited primary adoption service provider or approved
person (unless a limited exception applies), and that they are providing adoption services.[4]
Send the case to the corresponding U.S. government of fice to complete the orphan
determination.[5]
B. Burden and Standard of Proof
In matters involving immigration benefits, the petitioner always has the burden of proving eligibility for
the immigration benefit sought.[6] This determination may include questions of foreign law . When a
PAP relies on foreign law to establish eligibility for the beneficiary , the application of the foreign law is
a question of fact, which must be proved by the P AP. Therefore, the burden of proof is on the P AP to
establish that the child is eligible.[7]8/13/25, 3:55 AM Policy Manual | USCIS

3. Evidence of Arrivals to and Departures from the United States
Liberian principal applicants must provide a list of all arrivals to and departures from the United States.
All applicants also should submit any evidence of all arrivals to and departures from the United States.
Applicants should also submit any evidence showing residence from the date of his or her first arrival
where residence was established until the filing of the applicant’ s LRIF-based adjustment application.
USCIS uses this information to determine the date of lawful permanent residence for approved LRIF-
based adjustment applicants.[29]
Residence means the applicant’ s place of general abode; the place of general abode means his or her
principal, actual dwelling place in fact, without regard to intent.[30]

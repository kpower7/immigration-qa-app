In general, an applicant who is inadmissible to the United States may only obtain LPR status if he or
she obtains a waiver or other form of relief, if available.[23] In general, if a ground of inadmissibility
applies, an applicant must apply for a waiver or other form of relief to overcome that inadmissibility .
[24] If a waiver or other form of relief is granted, USCIS may approve the application to adjust status if
the applicant is otherwise eligible.

Technical Update - Child Citizenship Act and Children of U.S. Government
Employees Residing Abroad
July 20, 2015
This technical update clarifies that the child of a U.S. government employee temporarily stationed
abroad is considered to be residing in the United States for purposes of acquisition of citizenship
under INA  320.
Affected Sections
12 USCIS-PM H.4  - Chapter 4 - Automatic Acquisition of Citizenship after Birth (INA  320)
12 USCIS-PM K.2  - Chapter 2 - Certificate of Citizenship

C. Change of Status
Certain categories of nonimmigrants are eligible to change status to that of an H-3 nonimmigrant,
including certain students and other temporary visa holders.[6] Such change of status requests must
establish that: 
The beneficiaries entered the United States legally; 
The beneficiaries have never worked in the United States illegally , or otherwise violated the
terms of their visa; and 
The expiration date on the beneficiary’ s I-94 has not passed.[7]

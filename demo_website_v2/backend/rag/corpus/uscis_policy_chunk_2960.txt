Part G - Diversity Immigrant-Based Adjustment
Chapter 1 - Purpose and Background
A. Purpose
As part of the Immigration Act of 1990, Congress established the Diversity Immigrant V isa (DV)
Program.[1] In doing so, Congress created a new immigrant visa category specifically for aliens from
countries with lower rates of admission as immigrants to the United States.[2]
B. Background
The DV Program allows aliens from countries with low immigration rates to the United States to submit
an entry for a chance to apply for an immigrant visa in the next fiscal year . Congress established
guidelines to distribute immigrant visas in the DV category based on the immigration rates of specific
countries and regions to ensure a greater number of DVs are allocated to aliens from countries and
regions with lower immigration rates to the United States.[3]

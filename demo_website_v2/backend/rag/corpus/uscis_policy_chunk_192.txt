Chapter 7 - Privacy and Confidentiality
A. Privacy Act of 1974
The Privacy Act provides that federal agencies must protect against the unauthorized disclosure of
personally identifiable information (PII) that it collects, disseminates, uses, or maintains.[1] The Privacy
Act requires that personal information belonging to U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents
(LPRs) be protected from unauthorized disclosure. V iolations of these requirements may result in civil
and criminal penalties.

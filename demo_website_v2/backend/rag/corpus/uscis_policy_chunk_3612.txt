B. Discretionary Determination
When making a discretionary determination, the of ficer should review the entire record and give the
appropriate weight to each adverse and favorable factor . Once the of ficer has weighed each factor , the
officer should consider all of the factors cumulatively to determine whether the favorable factors
outweigh the unfavorable ones. If the of ficer determines that the positive factors outweigh the negative
factors, then the applicant merits a favorable exercise of discretion.

Chapter 3 - Documentation and Evidence
A. Filing Requirements
1. Canadian Citizens
Canadian citizens residing outside the United States may choose to file a Petition for a Nonimmigrant
Worker ( Form I-129 ) with USCIS requesting initial TN status. Canadian citizens may also file Form I-
129 if requesting an extension of status while currently in valid TN status, or if requesting a change of
status to TN from another valid nonimmigrant status. Canadian citizens usually do not need a visa as
a TN professional,[1] although the U.S. Department of State (DOS) can issue a visa at a U.S. embassy
or consulate to qualified Canadian TN visa applicants upon request. A Canadian citizen without a TN
visa may also apply for TN nonimmigrant status at a U.S. port of entry .[2]

Chapter 7 - Physical or Mental Disorder with Associated Harmful Behavior
A. Physical or Mental Disorders with Associated Harmful Behavior[1]
Applicants who have physical or mental disorders and harmful behavior associated with those
disorders are inadmissible.[2] The inadmissibility ground is divided into two subcategories:
Current physical or mental disorders, with associated harmful behavior . 
Past physical or mental disorders, with associated harmful behavior that is likely to recur or lead

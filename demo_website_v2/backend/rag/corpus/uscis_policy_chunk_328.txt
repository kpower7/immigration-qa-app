Saturday , Sunday , or federal holiday , USCIS applies the regulatory definition of day[35] and extends
the deadline for filing until the end of the next business day that is not a Saturday , Sunday , or federal
holiday .[36] In these situations, while the receipt date will continue to reflect the date USCIS physically
received the request, USCIS considers the benefit request to have been timely filed.[37]
The determination of whether a benefit request was timely submitted is not a criterion for acceptance;
rather , it is part of the eligibility determination for the benefit request that an of ficer makes at the time
of adjudication. Therefore, USCIS does not reject filings at intake due to missed deadlines.
USCIS considers electronically submitted benefit requests to be received immediately upon
submission. Therefore, electronic filings are not af fected by the fact that USCIS does not accept
deliveries on Saturdays, Sundays, or federal holidays, and USCIS does not apply the regulatory
definition of day to extend the filing period for benefit requests filed electronically .[38]

3. Humanitarian Fee W aivers 
USCIS provides fee exemptions for many forms filed by certain humanitarian categories of requestors.
[13] If not otherwise exempt from paying the fee, an individual may request a fee waiver for any
application or petition that is related to any of the following humanitarian categories:
Battered spouses of A, G, E-3, or H nonimmigrants;
Battered spouses or children of a lawful permanent resident or U.S. citizen under INA  Section
240A(b)(2);
T nonimmigrants;
Temporary Protected Status (TPS);
U nonimmigrants;
VAWA self-petitioners and derivative(s);
Conditional permanent residents (CPRs) filing a waiver of the joint filing requirement based on
battery or extreme cruelty;
Abused spouses and children adjusting status under the Cuban Adjustment Act (CAA) and

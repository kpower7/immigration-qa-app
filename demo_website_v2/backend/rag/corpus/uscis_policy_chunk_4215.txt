Chapter 5 - Conditional Permanent Resident Spouses and Naturalization
A. General Requirements for Conditional Permanent Residents
Since 1986, certain spouses of U.S. citizens have been admitted to the United States as lawful
permanent residents on a conditional basis for a period of 2 years.[1] In general, a conditional
permanent resident (CPR) must jointly file with his or her petitioning spouse a Petition to Remove
Conditions on Residence ( Form I-751 ) with USCIS during the 90-day period immediately preceding
the second anniversary of his or her admission as a CPR in order to remove the conditions.[2] An
approval of a petition to remove conditions demonstrates the bona fides of the marital relationship.
In order for USCIS to approve the petition to remove conditions, the CPR must establish that:
